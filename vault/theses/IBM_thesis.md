# IBM — Investment Thesis

## Style
Boring Fundamentals / Fear-Driven Dip-Buy

## Core Thesis
A value entry on a selloff Bill believes is overdone. Picked up from Stephanie Link's commentary: at a ~19 P/E, IBM is being priced for its execution stumbles, not for what it still is — a durable enterprise software and infrastructure franchise that CEO leadership frames as essential plumbing for how customers actually run AI workloads, not a speculative AI bet in its own right. The market has marked the stock down hard on a revenue guidance cut; the thesis is that the cut is a near-term execution problem, not evidence the core franchise is impaired.

IBM is also a founding participant in the newly formed Open Secure AI Alliance (AI-driven cyber-defense standards), which Bill reads as a data point that IBM is still positioning itself inside the AI infrastructure conversation rather than being displaced by it — supporting, not central to, the valuation case.

## Bull Case
- ~19 P/E prices in the execution missteps already; a reasonable multiple for a business with recurring software/services revenue, not a growth multiple that requires everything to go right.
- Management's framing — most of IBM's software is essential to running AI workloads, not competing to build the models — is a defensible moat if true: infrastructure picks-and-shovels exposure without foundation-model competitive risk.
- Founding member of the Open Secure AI Alliance: a signal IBM is staying relevant to enterprise AI security/infrastructure standards rather than being bypassed.
- Selloff + analyst price-target cuts already happened; the bad news is priced, which is the setup a dip-buying / fear-driven-discount style is built to use.

## Key Risks
- The revenue guidance cut and "execution missteps" the sentiment is based on could be the start of a trend, not a one-off — thesis assumes it's transient.
- Analyst sentiment has turned cautious post-miss; if guidance is cut again, the "overdone selloff" read is wrong and this becomes a value trap.
- "Essential for running AI" is management's framing, not yet demonstrated in revenue growth — the thesis is a bet on that framing holding up, not on a proof point that already exists.
- Mixed market sentiment cuts both ways: could mean the market is behind, or could mean the market sees something not yet visible in the P/E.
