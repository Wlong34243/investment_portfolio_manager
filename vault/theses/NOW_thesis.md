# NOW — Investment Thesis

## Style
Hyper-Growth SaaS / Enterprise Workflow / Legacy Compounder

## Core Thesis
**Conviction broken 2026-06. Scaling out.**

The original thesis was that ServiceNow is the "operating system" for the modern enterprise — near-zero churn, land-and-expand, and AI (Now Assist) monetized as a premium tier on top of entrenched workflows. That thesis assumed AI would be something ServiceNow *sells into* its moat.

I no longer believe that. The bet I am unwilling to make is that a seat-and-module-priced workflow vendor survives the AI transition intact. The same agentic capability that lets ServiceNow charge more for Pro/Plus also lets a customer collapse the workflow layer entirely — and the value of "we own the system of record for your IT tickets" falls the moment the tickets route themselves. Stickiness protects the incumbent against *displacement by another vendor*; it does not protect against the category shrinking. The renewal rate can stay at 98% while seats and modules per customer fall.

I am not claiming to know the timing, and I am not shorting the idea. I am declining to own a full position through the uncertainty. Scaling out in steps rather than dumping, because the moat is real and the unwind — if it happens — will be slow and visible.

## Prior Bull Case (retained for reference — no longer operative)
- **Incredible Stickiness:** Once an enterprise integrates ServiceNow to manage its internal IT and employee onboarding, it is practically impossible to rip out. This results in renewal rates consistently sitting around 98-99%.
- **The AI Monetization Catalyst:** ServiceNow is perfectly positioned to actually charge for AI. By embedding generative AI (Now Assist) into workflows, they save corporations thousands of man-hours, easily justifying the massive price hikes for their "Pro" and "Plus" tiers.
- **Land and Expand:** They are masters of upselling. A customer might start by buying the IT module, and three years later, they are paying for HR, legal, and customer service modules, structurally driving up the lifetime value of the account.
- **Margin Expansion:** As the massive, upfront sales and marketing costs required to acquire these Fortune 500 customers scale down relative to revenue, massive free cash flow is unleashed to the bottom line.

## Key Risks
- **AI disintermediation of the workflow layer (primary — this is the one that broke the thesis):** Agentic AI does not need to displace ServiceNow as a vendor to damage it. If work routes, triages and resolves itself, the seat-and-module pricing model deflates from inside an intact customer relationship. Renewal rate is the wrong metric to watch; seats-per-customer and modules-per-customer are the right ones.
- **Valuation / Multiple Compression:** Trades at a large premium to market. Any deceleration in top-line growth causes violent sell-offs — and the remaining lots no longer sit on a protective legacy basis.
- **Vendor Consolidation:** If Microsoft bundles competing workflow tools into Office 365, pricing power erodes. Now compounded by the AI risk above: bundling plus agentic substitution is a harsher combination than either alone.
- **Execution at Scale:** Past $10B+ revenue, avoiding bureaucratic slowdown is the core internal challenge.
