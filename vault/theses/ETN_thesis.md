# ETN — Investment Thesis

## Core Thesis

Thematic pick-and-shovel play on the AI data center build-out. Eaton is a solid supporter of the buildout — electrical equipment, power management, and grid infrastructure that data centers need regardless of which AI compute vendor wins. Position is about market position in power infrastructure, not a deep single-company conviction call.

## Bull Case
- Direct beneficiary of AI/data-center capex regardless of which chipmaker or hyperscaler wins the underlying compute race.
- Power management and electrical distribution are a bottleneck for data center build-out, giving pricing power to established suppliers.

## Key Risks
- Highly linked to the pace of AI/data-center capex; a broad slowdown in that build-out hits this thesis directly.
- As a thematic satellite (not core conviction), sized and trimmed opportunistically rather than held through deep drawdowns.

## Position Sizing & Action Zones
Thematic Specialist bucket — market-position bet on AI infrastructure buildout, not a GARP-style quality/valuation call.

## Exit Conditions
- AI/data-center capex cycle clearly rolls over for multiple quarters.
- A more direct or better-valued power-infrastructure/AI-buildout vehicle emerges.
