# SPCX — Investment Thesis

## Style
Thematic Moonshot / Space Infrastructure

## Core Thesis
A moonshot on space data centers and international telecom. The bet: compute is becoming power- and cooling-constrained on Earth while launch costs collapse, making orbital data centers and satellite-delivered connectivity a plausible next infrastructure layer — and space-based telecom can leapfrog terrestrial buildout in markets that never got full fiber or 5G. Sized as a moonshot: small enough to lose entirely, kept for the asymmetric payoff if the theme matures.

## Entry Context
Smallest position in the book (~0.3%), currently carrying a meaningful percentage loss — expected behavior for a moonshot, and irrelevant to the thesis at this size. This is a lottery-ticket allocation from the theme budget, not a scaling candidate.

## Bull Case
- AI power demand collides with terrestrial grid constraints; orbit offers solar power and free cooling.
- Launch cost deflation keeps expanding what is economical to put in space.
- Satellite direct-to-device telecom opens underserved international markets.
- Optionality: any one credible commercial proof point re-rates the whole theme.

## Key Risks
- Technology risk: orbital data centers are largely unproven at commercial scale (radiation, bandwidth, servicing).
- Capital destruction is the base case for most moonshot cohorts; dilution and failures en route.
- Long timeline — the theme may be real but a decade early.
- Position is small enough that even a win moves the portfolio little; that is the accepted trade.
