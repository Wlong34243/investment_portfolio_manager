# LLY — Investment Thesis

## Style
GARP / Quality Rotation Pick

## Core Thesis
Quality rotation pick. Lilly is the premier franchise in the biggest drug market ever created (GLP-1 metabolic disease) with a pipeline that extends the runway — Alzheimer's, oncology, next-generation incretins. Entered as part of rotating capital from crowded AI-creator names toward quality healthcare, the sector repeatedly flagged as the next AI adopter (drug discovery, trial efficiency).

## Entry Context
Starter position (~1%) with room to scale. The healthcare-as-AI-adopter theme (The Compound, Moyo) plus the GLP-1 franchise made LLY the single-name expression alongside the XBI basket.

## Bull Case
- GLP-1 market still supply-constrained with expanding indications (cardio, sleep apnea, kidney).
- Oral incretins would widen the moat against compounders and late entrants.
- AI-accelerated discovery benefits the company with the deepest data and pipeline.
- Quality compounder character: pricing power, margins, pipeline depth.

## Key Risks
- Premium valuation: priced as the franchise; any GLP-1 stumble compresses the multiple hard.
- Competition: Novo and a wave of next-gen entrants; pricing pressure as supply normalizes.
- Political drug-pricing risk on the most visible expensive drug category in America.
- Pipeline binary events move the stock violently in both directions.

## Exit Conditions
- GLP-1 franchise leadership lost to a superior competitor product.
- Drug-pricing legislation that structurally caps the metabolic franchise.
- Valuation premium expands beyond what quality-at-reasonable-price tolerates — trim, don't exit.
