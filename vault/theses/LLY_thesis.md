## Bull Case
- GLP-1 market still supply-constrained with expanding indications (cardio, sleep apnea, kidney).
- Oral incretins would widen the moat against compounders and late entrants.
- AI-accelerated discovery benefits the company with the deepest data and pipeline.
- Quality compounder character: pricing power, margins, pipeline depth.

## Key Risks
- Premium valuation: priced as the franchise; any GLP-1 stumble compresses the multiple hard.
- Competition: Novo and a wave of next-gen entrants; pricing pressure as supply normalizes.
- Political drug-pricing risk on the most visible expensive drug category in America.
- Pipeline binary events move the stock violently in both directions.
