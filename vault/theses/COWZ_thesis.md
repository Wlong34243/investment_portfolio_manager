# COWZ — Investment Thesis

## Style
Value ETF / Tech-Exposure Counterweight

## Core Thesis
A value selection balanced against the portfolio's tech exposure. COWZ screens the Russell 1000 for free-cash-flow yield — owning the businesses that generate cash today rather than promise it later. Its role is structural: the portfolio carries heavy AI/tech beta through QQQM and single names, and COWZ is the deliberate counterweight that benefits when the market rotates from growth to value.

## Entry Context
Held as ballast with a factor tilt rather than a directional bet. Cash-cow companies (energy, healthcare, industrials typically dominate the screen) tend to hold up when long-duration growth de-rates.

## Bull Case
- Free-cash-flow yield is the factor that works when rates stay higher for longer.
- Direct beneficiary of the AI-unwind rotation into value and "real businesses."
- Rules-based rebalancing keeps it pointed at whatever sector currently generates cash.

## Key Risks
- Lags badly in momentum-led bull markets — the cost of the counterweight role.
- Sector concentration drifts with the screen (often energy-heavy); check look-through overlap with XOM/ET.
- Value traps: high FCF yield sometimes means the market correctly sees decline.
