# AMZN — Investment Thesis

## Core Thesis

GARP-by-intuition, anchored in daily product use. AWS is the stable, durable earnings engine — long-term entrenched enterprise cloud infrastructure, now extending into next-generation data center networking (including space-based/optical links between data centers) and AI compute build-out. Retail and Prime are the flywheel that keeps me a daily customer and gives me firsthand conviction in the business, not just the numbers.

## Bull Case
- AWS: durable, high-margin cloud backbone with real switching costs for enterprise customers.
- AI integration across AWS (Bedrock/Trainium-class infra) and internal operations extends the cloud moat rather than threatening it.
- Emerging data-center networking investment (including space/optical links) is a forward-looking capacity and latency edge.
- Daily personal use of Amazon retail/Prime is a live, ongoing product-conviction check on the consumer side.

## Key Risks
- AWS growth deceleration or share loss to Azure/GCP would remove the core earnings pillar.
- Heavy, sustained AI infrastructure capex without matching revenue traction would pressure margins.
- Retail remains lower-margin and competitive; it's the flywheel, not the profit engine.

## Position Sizing & Action Zones
GARP-by-intuition core holding — sized and scaled like the rest of the GARP bucket, not a satellite/thematic bet.

## Exit Conditions
- AWS loses structural share or margin leadership in enterprise cloud.
- Product conviction breaks — the "I use and love it daily" premise underlying this thesis no longer holds.
- A clearly better GARP opportunity emerges with less capex intensity for similar quality.
