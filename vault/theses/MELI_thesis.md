# MELI — Investment Thesis

## Style
Thematic Specialist / Latin American Tech

## Core Thesis
Latin American tech exposure through the region's dominant platform. MercadoLibre is the market-position bet: e-commerce, payments (Mercado Pago), and logistics moats across Brazil, Mexico, and Argentina, bought for its regional dominance rather than a valuation case. It is the single-stock expression of the same Western Hemisphere / LatAm bloc theme that EWZ and parts of EMXC express at the index level.

## Entry Context
Sized near the 3% thematic ceiling. The bet is that LatAm digital commerce and fintech penetration are still early relative to developed markets, and MELI owns the rails.

## Bull Case
- Dominant share in e-commerce and digital payments across the region's largest economies.
- Fintech (credit, wallet) expands take rate on an installed commerce base.
- Benefits from the US-fostered Western Hemisphere trade-bloc thesis (near-shoring capital flows into LatAm).

## Key Risks
- Currency: earns in BRL/ARS/MXN — devaluations hit dollar results even when the business performs.
- Argentina policy volatility and Brazil fiscal risk are recurring shocks.
- Competition from Amazon, Shein/Temu-style entrants, and local banks in payments.
- Overlap: EWZ and EMXC carry correlated LatAm exposure; check combined weight before adding.

## Scaling State
next_step: hold

## Rotation Priority
priority: medium

## Exit Conditions
- Loss of payments or commerce share to a funded competitor (market-position thesis broken).
- Regional currency crisis that structurally impairs dollar earnings power.
- Position drifts materially above the thematic ceiling on appreciation — trim per small-step discipline.
