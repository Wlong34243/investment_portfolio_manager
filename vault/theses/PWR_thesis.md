## Core Thesis
Quanta Services (PWR) serves as a critical enabler in the physical "AI food chain." While public market focus remains heavily weighted toward chips and software, the primary bottleneck for scaling artificial intelligence is physical power and power grid infrastructure. PWR provides essential electrical line, grid, and data center infrastructure solutions required to sustain high-density AI buildouts. Supported by strong fundamental balance sheet execution and consistent earnings performance, the stock provides direct operational exposure to enterprise CapEx expansion.
