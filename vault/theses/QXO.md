# QXO — Investment Thesis

## Style
GARP-by-intuition. Framework preference noted as Catalyst / Roll-up (Management Conviction) — CEO track record and a discounted entry point, not classic multiple-vs-growth GARP, but sized and ceiling-governed as GARP per your call.

## Core Thesis
Position initiated to ride the building-products distribution roll-up run by CEO Brad Jacobs. Stock is ~30% off its 52-week high, largely on market indigestion following the $17B TopBuild acquisition (closed July 1, 2026). The pullback is the entry point; the August 13 earnings call is the near-term catalyst where management is expected to lay out synergy timeline and integration progress for the combined ~$18B+ revenue entity, with an expected price bump if the narrative lands.

## Entry Context
Bought 225 shares @ $15.8278 (cost basis $3,563.00) on 2026-08-10. Funded by trimming 1 share of MELI and a fractional VTI sale. Rationale: Brad Jacobs' track record (built XPO into a logistics giant), a recommendation from Stephanie Link, and TopBuild adding to a portfolio that already includes Beacon Roofing Supply and Kodiak Building Partners.

## Bull Case
* **"XPO Playbook" applied to building materials** — Jacobs running the same M&A/operational-discipline framework in a fragmented industry.
* **Scale** — TopBuild makes QXO the #2 publicly traded building-products distributor in North America, adding an 18% adjusted-EBITDA-margin insulation business.
* **Cross-sell synergy target** — $300M projected by 2030 across Beacon/Kodiak/TopBuild, plus procurement and logistics efficiencies.
* **Data-center tailwind** — expanded scale positions QXO for large, multi-product AI-driven data-center construction jobs.

## Key Risks
* **Debt/dilution overhang** — TopBuild was 45% cash / 55% stock; market is pricing in balance-sheet and dilution risk.
* **Integration load** — Beacon (~$11B) + Kodiak ($2.25B) + TopBuild (~$17B) combined inside one year is a lot of moving parts.
* **Valuation** — TopBuild paid near 20% over its 60-day average; some analysts see limited room for further margin expansion.
* **Macro sensitivity** — building products/construction is rate- and housing-cycle exposed.
