# ZETA — Investment Thesis (New 2026-05-18)

## Style
Speculative AI Play / AI-Native Martech

## Honest Framing Up Front
Sourced from a Claude-on-X recommendation. That's a meaningfully different sourcing path than the GARP core or even the Stephanie Link channel — it's lower-confidence, higher-noise, and I should size it accordingly. This is a **speculative position**, not a core holding. I'm writing this thesis with that understood, and the sizing rules reflect it.

## Core Thesis: AI-Native Customer Data and Marketing Activation

Zeta Global is a customer data platform plus a marketing activation layer, built around a proprietary identity graph of 240M+ US individuals and an AI-driven decisioning engine that determines what to show whom, when, and on which channel. The thesis is that **as AI agents become the new interface between consumers and brands, the brands that can feed those agents structured customer intelligence will win, and Zeta wants to be the layer that supplies it**.

That's a real thesis. The execution risk is high, the competitive landscape is crowded (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Adobe, Braze, plus a long tail of CDPs), and the company has historically had to defend its accounting and identity graph quality. But if AI-native customer data activation becomes a real category — and there are reasons to think it might — Zeta is positioned earlier than most.

## Why I'm Sizing This Small

I'm explicit: this is a speculative AI play, not a high-conviction core position. The sizing rules:

- **Current allocation: 1.23%.** Starter size, appropriate for a speculative thesis.
- **Target weight: 1.5-2.0%.** Don't take this above 2%.
- **Hard cap: 2.0%.** Lower than other thematic names because the conviction is lower and the volatility is higher.
- **Drawdown tolerance: 50%.** I should expect this to halve at some point in a tech-led selloff. If I'm not OK with that, the position should be smaller or closed.

The discipline matters because the temptation with speculative AI names is to add on strength when they're working ("it's running, must be right"). I'm pre-committing to: I add on weakness, not strength.

## Bull Case Drivers

- **AI-driven decisioning is real product, not just narrative.** Zeta's platform actually makes per-message decisions on what to send whom across channels, and customers report measurable lift.
- **Identity graph is a moat.** The data asset is hard to replicate; size matters in identity resolution.
- **AI agent buildout creates new demand surface.** As brands deploy AI customer-service and sales agents, those agents need access to customer history, preference signals, and channel-appropriate creative. Zeta wants to be that supply layer.
- **Smaller-cap re-rating potential.** Unlike mega-cap AI names, Zeta has room for meaningful multiple expansion if the thesis hits.

## Key Risks (which I am sizing for, not waving away)

- **Crowded competitive landscape** — Salesforce, Adobe, and others have larger distribution and deeper enterprise relationships.
- **Identity graph quality scrutiny** — Zeta has had short reports historically questioning the quality and provenance of its data. Real or not, the perception risk is permanent.
- **Customer concentration / churn** — martech contracts can churn fast in budget pressure cycles.
- **Multiple compression on growth dec** — at any meaningful multiple, a deceleration would re-rate the stock hard.
- **Speculative AI re-rate risk** — if AI narrative cools, smaller-cap AI plays get hit first and hardest.
- **Recommendation source quality** — this came from social media, which is the lowest-confidence sourcing channel I use. Position size reflects that.

## Hard Exit Conditions

1. Revenue growth drops below 25% for two consecutive quarters — the hypergrowth thesis is the entire reason to own this; without it, there's no thesis.
2. Customer churn accelerates materially or a top-10 customer departs.
3. New short report or accounting issue surfaces that I can't quickly assess as noise.
4. Position falls below 0.5% on price action — too small to matter; either rebuild or close.
5. Better AI-themed risk-reward emerges where my speculative slot is better deployed.

## Why This Earns a Slot

Two reasons:

1. **AI exposure outside mega-cap tech.** My mega-tech/AI complex is 30% even post-rebalance. ZETA gives me AI thesis exposure in a small-cap, different-correlation profile. If the AI thesis broadens beyond mega-cap winners, this benefits.

2. **Asymmetric payoff structure.** At 1-2% sizing, the worst case is a manageable loss; the best case is a 3-5x recovery on a small-cap re-rate. That's an acceptable speculation in the satellite sleeve.

- Add bias: on weakness, never on strength.
- Trim bias: on cap breach (>2%) or hard-exit trigger.
