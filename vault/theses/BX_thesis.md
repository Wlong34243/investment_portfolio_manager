# BX — Investment Thesis

## Core Thesis (Alternative Asset Management Compounder)

I am buying the dominant global alternative asset manager at a moment when the asset class itself is in the early innings of a structural expansion. Blackstone manages over $1 trillion in AUM across private equity, real estate, credit, and infrastructure — and is aggressively opening alternative assets to retail and wealth-channel investors, a market that remains largely untapped. The thesis is that Blackstone earns fee-related earnings (FRE) that compound with AUM growth, layered with performance-driven realizations that can generate lumpy but material upside when credit and PE cycles turn favorable.

This is not a pure financial bet on the rate cycle — Blackstone has structurally repositioned toward perpetual capital vehicles (BREIT, BCRED, BX Infrastructure) that collect management fees regardless of exits. The franchise is widening its moat precisely when competitors without brand or scale are struggling to raise capital in a tighter LP environment.

## Entry Context & Valuation (Lynch GARP Lens)

Cost basis: ~$125.53 per share. At entry, BX was trading below its peak amid concerns about commercial real estate (BREIT redemptions), higher-for-longer rates compressing PE exit markets, and general risk-off in financial stocks. The market was pricing a cyclically impaired earnings picture; I am paying for normalized FRE compounding plus an embedded call option on the rate cycle and capital markets reopening.

- GARP frame: FRE alone justifies a mid-to-high-20s multiple; realizations and carry add to that. At entry the blended multiple was reasonable for a business compounding AUM at 15–20% annually.
- Key insight: The wealth-channel buildout (retail alternatives) is the most underappreciated growth driver. Penetrating even 5% of the retail liquid alternatives market materially grows the AUM base beyond institutional fundraising cycles.

## Bull Case

- **Perpetual capital expansion:** BREIT, BCRED, and BX Infrastructure together represent a growing base of fee-generating AUM that is not subject to vintage-year exit pressure.
- **Wealth channel optionality:** Blackstone has partnered with wirehouses and RIAs to distribute alternatives to HNW and mass-affluent investors. This channel is still in early growth and could become the single largest source of new AUM over five years.
- **Rate cycle tailwind:** As rates moderate, PE exit markets reopen, BREIT NAV pressure eases, and credit spread widening reverses — creating a realizations cycle that can dramatically boost distributable earnings.
- **Scale moat in fundraising:** LP relationships, brand, and track record compound. Smaller managers get squeezed in a tighter LP budget environment; Blackstone takes share.
- **Infrastructure and credit growth:** The infrastructure and private credit sleeves are the fastest-growing segments and carry lower cyclicality than equity PE.

## Behavioral Guardrails & What I Watch

- Drawdown tolerance: accept 25–30% drawdowns on rate fears or BREIT-related headlines as long as FRE trajectory and AUM growth remain intact.
- Watch: quarterly FRE per share growth, BREIT/BCRED net inflows, fundraising pace by strategy, and realization environment.
- I will not anchor on distributable earnings in quarters with few exits — FRE is the durable signal.

## Hard Exit Conditions

1. Structural regulatory changes restrict retail access to private alternatives, capping the wealth-channel opportunity.
2. FRE growth stalls for more than four consecutive quarters without a clear cyclical explanation (suggesting the business is scaling into AUM growth but not earnings growth).
3. Blackstone's brand suffers a reputational event serious enough to impair LP relationships or retail distribution access.
4. A clearly superior financial compounder emerges offering better FRE growth, capital return, and optionality at a lower valuation.

## Scaling State & Priority

- Next step: build toward 3–4% as rate cycle clarity improves and realization environment opens.
- Rotation priority: medium; current 0.75% is a starter position with room to grow.
